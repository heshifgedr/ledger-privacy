English简体中文

What Ledger does

Ledger is an independent expense-tracking app. You can enter transactions manually and optionally enable notification capture for selected WeChat Pay and Alipay sources. No payment-account login is required.

Information accessed and stored on your device

After your affirmative consent and Android notification-access permission, Ledger accesses notification content from your selected payment sources to identify supported payment, receipt, transfer and refund alerts. Android notification access can expose notifications beyond those sources; Ledger filters sources before parsing. Unrelated notifications and ordinary chat contents are not stored.

Notification text is processed in memory. Ledger stores transaction fields such as amount, currency, merchant, date, category, source, review status, notes, refund links and one-way hashes used for duplicate detection. It also stores your categories, merchant-category preferences and app settings. Raw notification text is not stored in the ledger database or application logs.

Purpose

These fields are used to create and edit your ledger, identify possible duplicates, link refunds and calculate summaries on your device. Notification capture can be paused in Ledger or disabled in Android Settings. Pausing capture keeps existing ledger entries.

Transmission and third parties

This version has no Internet permission and includes no advertising, analytics or cloud-recognition SDKs. The app does not send notification or ledger data to the developer's servers. It does not sell personal data.

You may explicitly export a CSV or encrypted backup through Android's file picker. The destination may be a local or third-party storage provider you select. If you choose a cloud provider, that provider handles the file under its own terms and privacy policy. CSV exports are not encrypted.

Storage, security and retention

The ledger database is stored in Android app-private storage and is not itself an encrypted database. Android automatic cloud backup and device migration are disabled for the app. In-app password-protected backups use PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 and AES-256-GCM. Backup passwords are not saved and cannot be recovered.

Ledger entries remain until you delete them, clear the ledger, clear app storage or uninstall the app. Clear ledger removes entries and merchant rules and pauses capture; category definitions and display preferences may remain. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes app-local data. Exported files remain wherever you saved them until you delete them using that storage provider.

Your choices

You can use manual entry without notification access, select or disable payment sources, pause capture, edit or delete entries, export your ledger or clear local app data. Ledger does not create a user account.

中文说明

拾账是独立记账工具，可以手动记录交易。只有你主动同意并开启 Android 通知访问后，才会处理所选微信支付和支付宝来源中支持的支付、收款、转账及退款通知。Android 通知访问可能接触其他通知，应用在解析前筛选来源，不保存无关通知或普通聊天内容。

通知原文仅在内存中用于识别，不写入账本数据库或应用日志。应用在本机保存金额、币种、商户、时间、分类、来源、状态、备注、退款关系、不可逆去重摘要，以及自定义分类、商户分类规则和设置，用于记账、核对、去重及汇总。

本版本没有网络权限，不含广告、分析或云端识别 SDK，不向开发者服务器上传通知或账单，不出售个人数据。你可通过系统文件选择器主动导出 CSV 或加密备份；选择云盘等第三方提供方时，该提供方按照自己的条款和隐私政策处理文件。CSV 为明文。

数据库保存在 Android 应用私有空间，本身未采用数据库加密。应用已关闭 Android 自动云备份及设备迁移；应用内加密备份使用 PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 与 AES-256-GCM，密码不保存，遗失后无法恢复。

账单保留至你删除、清空账本、清除应用存储或卸载。清空账本会删除账单与商户规则、暂停自动记账，但分类定义和显示偏好可能保留。清除应用存储或卸载会删除应用本地数据。导出的文件仍保存在你选择的位置，需要另行删除。

你可以不授权通知访问而只用手动记账，也可随时选择来源、暂停捕获、编辑或删除记录、导出数据或清除本地数据。本应用不创建用户账号。
